Privacy Policy

(Last modified: December 2023)

In accordance with the Regulation (EU) 2016/679, General Data Protection, in this Privacy Policy we inform you about:

  • Who is responsible for the processing of your data
  • What data we collect
  • Purpose
  • How and where information is shared
  • Security of your data
  • Rights and access to information

1. Information Collection

In the course of providing digital innovation and software services, we may collect:

  • Identity & Contact: Name, professional email, company details provided via inquiry forms.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and usage patterns during your visit to our platforms.
  • Project Data: Requirements and specifications shared for the development of bespoke digital solutions.

2. Use of Data

Your data is used strictly for the purpose of service delivery and improvement:

  • To manage client relationships and project workflows.
  • To optimize and secure our digital infrastructure.
  • To communicate updates regarding our technology stack and innovative solutions.
  • To comply with regulatory and legal framework obligations.

3. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard encryption and security protocols to safeguard your information. Data is stored on secure servers located within the EEA, and access is strictly limited to personnel required for service execution.

4. Your Rights

As a data subject under GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or request the erasure of your personal data. You may also object to processing or request data portability at any time by contacting us directly.
