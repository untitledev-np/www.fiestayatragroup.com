Cookie Policy

This policy details how Fiestayatra Group SL uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your digital experience and provide seamless functionality across our platforms.

1. Understanding Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that allow our servers to recognize your browser. They are essential for security, performance optimization, and personalized user experiences within our digital infrastructure.

2. Categorization of Use

We utilize cookies for the following primary functions:

  • Infrastructure Essentials: Critical for navigation and the core operation of our web ecosystems.
  • Performance Analytics: Aggregated data used to understand interaction patterns and refine our software solutions.
  • User Preferences: Storing configuration settings to provide a consistent cross-session experience.
